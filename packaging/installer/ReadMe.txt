Platinum Reality — Important Information

Platinum Reality is a desktop for Arch Linux that looks and works like Mac OS 9's
Platinum appearance: a menu bar, the Control Strip, windows with title bars you can
collapse, and a Finder.

Before you install

• The installer erases the whole disk you choose. Everything on it will be lost,
  including other operating systems. Back up anything you want to keep first.
• Your computer must start up with UEFI. Platinum Reality uses systemd-boot.
• You do not need an internet connection to install. Afterwards, connect to Wi-Fi
  from the Control Strip and keep the system up to date with: sudo pacman -Syu

What you get

• Arch Linux with the Platinum Reality desktop, chosen automatically when you log in.
• Firefox, Konsole and the usual Arch tools. KDE Plasma is not installed.
• Apple's own icons, sounds and fonts are not included. Platinum Reality draws its own.
  If you own a Mac OS 9 installation, the Appearance control panel can import its
  pictures and sounds for your own use.
